About me

I'm Prince Kyle Beringia, also known as KINYTDEV. I have nearly five years of experience in game development, mainly working on Roblox projects. I've also worked in development leadership, helping teams coordinate their work and move projects forward.

I've worked with three different Philippine National Police roleplay development groups and contributed to the development of two PNP roleplay games. I currently continue working as Lead Developer at Empire Eclipse Games, where I've been for almost three years.

Alongside game development, I've spent around two years learning ethical hacking and cybersecurity. I enjoy understanding how systems work, solving problems, building game systems, and learning how software and networks can be made more secure.

I believe good development comes from steady practice, teamwork, curiosity, and being willing to keep learning.
